# Autoresearch Cycle 2 — 2026-06-20

Two genuine, backtestable improvement attempts on top of the cycle-1 candidate (V8 Elo-blend
logit). Same fixed evaluator, same folds, same governance: select on DEV+GATE (2018+2022),
never tune to the locked 2026 fold, no fold regression > 0.001.

## 2a — Improve the dominant feature: Elo construction
Harness: `scripts/elo_research.py` (rebuilds Elo per config, reuses `runner.evaluate_fold`).
Hypothesis: match-importance weighting (friendlies count less, World Cup more), and tuned
K / home-advantage, improve Elo and therefore every fold.

| Elo config | DEV+GATE | 2018 | 2022 | 2026 (locked) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **E0 current flat K=40, ha=65** | **0.3892** | 0.3591 | 0.4194 | 0.4160 |
| E6 flat K=40, ha=100 | 0.3903 | 0.3615 | 0.4192 | 0.4229 |
| E4 weighted K=30 | 0.3933 | 0.3728 | 0.4137 | 0.4038 |
| E1 weighted K=40 | 0.3950 | 0.3720 | 0.4180 | 0.3956 |
| E2 weighted K=50 | 0.3979 | 0.3722 | 0.4237 | 0.3844 |

**Decision: REVERT (keep flat K=40, ha=65).** Importance weighting *worsens* the selection
folds. It does improve the locked 2026 fold (E2 0.384 vs 0.416) — but selecting on that would
be tuning to the holdout, which is forbidden. Negative result; Elo is already near-optimal.

## 2b — Add new backtestable signal: FIFA world ranking
New data: `scripts/fetch_fifa_rankings.py` → Dato-Futbol/fifa-ranking (open, 1992–2024 monthly).
Integrated time-safely (asof `release_date <= kickoff`, `tolerance=None`) →
`fifa_points_z_*`, `fifa_rank_percentile_*`, `fifa_z_delta`, `fifa_rank_pct_delta`. Coverage
75/75 WC teams; age 15–51 days for 1998–2022 (fresh), **635 days for 2026 (stale → flagged
`fifa_is_stale`)** because the dataset ends 2024-09. Harness: `scripts/fifa_research.py`.

| Candidate variant | DEV+GATE | 2018 | 2022 | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **W0 accepted (no FIFA)** | **0.3892** | 0.3591 | 0.4194 | baseline |
| W1 + FIFA deltas | 0.3881 | 0.3527 | 0.4235 | improves 2018, **regresses 2022** |
| W3 FIFA-only (B2) | 0.3870 | 0.3857 | 0.3884 | improves 2022, **regresses 2018 badly** |
| W4 Elo+FIFA lean | 0.3940 | 0.3624 | 0.4256 | worse |

**Decision: REVERT (no FIFA in the candidate).** Every FIFA variant either misses the 0.2%
bar or trades one selection fold for the other — no robust improvement. FIFA is 0.78-correlated
with the internal Elo (little independent signal) and is stale for 2026. The earlier
"mean-only" pick of W3 was rejected once the no-regression rule was applied.

**Kept anyway:** the FIFA *data* is integrated and powers a genuine **baseline B2 (FIFA-only)**
— dev composite **0.3985**, clearly weaker than Elo (B1 0.3553), which honestly confirms Elo's
superiority. Interesting nuance: B2 is actually the *best* model on the 2022 gate (0.3884), so
FIFA may help once a fresher release is available.

## Net effect on the production model
**No change** — both experiments reverted per discipline. The official fixed-evaluator summary
composite is **0.398** (improved from 0.414 earlier purely via the football-data authoritative
2026 data, not via the candidate). 45 tests pass. The candidate ignores the new FIFA columns.

## Cumulative experiment count and the plateau
Cycle 1 (12 candidate variants) + cycle 2a (7 Elo configs) + cycle 2b (5 FIFA variants) = **24
well-designed experiments**. The robust DEV+GATE objective has plateaued around 0.389. Per the
governance "stop a branch after repeated non-improvement and identify the missing information":

**The binding constraint is data, not modeling.** The two highest-value unmet needs:
1. **Timestamped HISTORICAL odds** (2018/2022) → a *backtestable* market-anchored model and CLV.
   The Odds API free tier gives live odds only; historical needs a paid plan or another source.
2. **Fresher / forward strength data for 2026** (FIFA release closer to the tournament, squad
   value, availability) → the 2026 features currently lean on a 2024-09 FIFA release + Elo.

## Recommendation for cycle 3
Do not keep micro-tuning the candidate on free historical data — it is at its ceiling. The
next real gain requires (a) historical odds to make the market benchmark backtestable, or
(b) a fresher 2026 strength signal. Until then, the live market consensus remains the best
forecast and the model's job is prospective scoring against it.
