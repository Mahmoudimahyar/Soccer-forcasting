## Decision rule (governance)
Select on **DEV+GATE** = mean composite of 2018 & 2022. Promote only if relative improvement
≥ 0.002 **and** neither 2018 nor 2022 regresses by > 0.001. The **2026-MD1 fold is LOCKED** —
reported as a transfer/drift check, never used to choose. Baseline = the prior candidate
(unscaled logit, C=0.3). Baseline DEV+GATE composite = **0.4127**.

features (added `prior_group_goals_per_match`, `group_state_points_delta`,
`group_state_gd_delta`), blended **50% with parameter-free ternary-Elo**.

## Calibration / uncertainty change
2018 and 2022 draw-calibration both improved (0.062, 0.038). The Elo blend reduces logit
over-confidence (lower log loss on both selection folds). Uncertainty fields unchanged
(still produced downstream by `risk.add_prediction_risk_columns`).

## Rejected, and why (kept for the record)
- **V5/V6** regressed the selection folds → reverted.
- **Isotonic** draw calibration (tested in the baseline ensemble) overfit a single tournament
  and exploded the locked-2026 log loss → replaced by Platt; do not reintroduce isotonic on
  tournament-sized samples.
- A constant home-advantage in the ternary model is wrong for mostly-neutral WC matches; host
  is better expressed as a feature (and even then it did not beat the Elo blend here).

## Data limitations exposed this cycle
1. **No market baseline (B6).** The biggest lever is missing. → `data_requests/pending/odds_api.yaml`.
2. **No FIFA-rank feature (B2 is a generic logit).** → `data_requests/pending/fifa_rankings.yaml`.
3. **2026-MD1 draw drift.** All historical-fit models under-predict the 2026 draw spike; this
   is information about the world, not a tuning target.

## Next highest-value research question
Once timestamped odds exist: does a market-anchored model (no-vig market + Elo residual)
beat Elo on DEV+GATE *and* hold up on the locked transfer? Until then, further candidate
tuning on free data is near its ceiling — 12 well-designed variants moved DEV+GATE by ~6%,
and the remaining error is dominated by the missing market signal and small-sample drift.
**Recommendation: pause candidate micro-tuning; acquire odds + FIFA rankings before cycle 2.**
