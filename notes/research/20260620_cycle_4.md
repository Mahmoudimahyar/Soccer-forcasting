# Autoresearch Cycle 4 — Can we beat the market? (2026-06-20)

Goal: improve the forecast until it **beats the no-vig market consensus** on out-of-sample
probability quality. The blocker was data (one fold of WC odds). The paid Odds API plan let us
fix that.

## The data unlock
`scripts/fetch_intl_odds.py` pulled near-closing (~90 min pre-kickoff) no-vig consensus for
**347 senior men's international matches, 2021–2025** across Euro 2020/2024, Copa 2024, AFCON
2024, Gold Cup 2025, WC 2022, **Nations League (130)**, and qualifiers. Joined to martj42 results
+ time-safe Elo: `scripts/build_intl_dataset.py` → `intl_market_dataset.csv` (341 matched).
This is a real, leakage-safe market dataset — every market line is pre-kickoff, every Elo is
`elo_before(<kickoff)`. ~4,800 of 20,000 credits used.

## The test (`scripts/test_beat_market.py`) — expanding-window temporal CV, 4 OOS folds
Models vs the raw market benchmark (composite = 0.40·RPS+0.25·LogLoss+0.20·drawBrier+0.15·drawCal):

| model | composite | vs market | folds beating mkt |
|---|---|---|---|
| market_raw | 0.3555 | — | — |
| **market + Elo blend** | **0.3383** | **+4.84%** | **4/4** |
| stacked logit (market+Elo) | 0.3693 | −3.86% | 2/4 |
| market draw-recalibration | 0.3783 | −6.40% | 0/4 |

**The simple market+Elo blend beats the market on every fold** (RPS 0.177→0.165, LogLoss
0.927→0.887). The *complex* models (stacked logit, draw recalibration) overfit and lose — the
anti-overfit signature that makes the blend credible.

## Scrutiny (`scripts/diagnose_blend.py`, `scripts/blend_variants.py`)
1. **Not a weight-fit artifact:** the fixed-weight sweep improves monotonically 0→0.5
   (+1.5% → +5.1%); no knife-edge.
2. **Simplest base wins:** ternary-Elo blend (+4.84%) > fitted-Elo-logit blend (+2.69%) >
   depth-aware (+3.73%). Added complexity reduces the edge.
3. **Edge scales with market noise:** thin books +4.4%, deep books (15+) +1.2%. Much of the
   edge is Elo *denoising* a median-of-EU-books consensus.
4. **World Cup (deep market, the 2026-relevant case):** beats the market by **+2.4%** at fixed
   w=0.3 and **+6.2%** pooled-OOS with train-fit weight (composite 0.396→0.371).

## Verdict — a reasonable, validated improvement over the market
On out-of-sample probability quality, **market + ~0.4·Elo robustly beats the no-vig consensus**
by ~3–5% composite overall and ~2–6% on the World Cup. This is the answer to "can we beat the
market": yes, modestly and robustly, on forecast quality.

### Honest caveats (must be read with the headline)
- It beats *our constructed* median-of-EU-books consensus at T-90min. The edge shrinks on
  deeper markets, so against the single sharpest closing line (e.g. Pinnacle close) it would be
  smaller — part of the gain is denoising, not pure alpha.
- ~2–6% probability-quality improvement is **not** proven betting profitability: the bookmaker
  overround on 1X2 (~5–7%) likely exceeds it. This is a *forecasting* win, not a +EV-vs-vig claim.
- 341 matches, few per competition; the prospective 2026 loop will keep testing it live.

## Angles tried that did NOT beat the simple blend (recorded)
Stacked multinomial logit, market draw-recalibration (Platt), fitted-Elo-logit base,
depth-aware weighting — all underperformed the simple blend out-of-sample. On 341 matches,
adding feature complexity (weather/stakes/rest stacks) risks overfitting more than it helps;
the robust, defensible model is the parameter-light market+Elo blend.
