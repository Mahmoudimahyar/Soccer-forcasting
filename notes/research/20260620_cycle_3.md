# Autoresearch Cycle 3 — 2026-06-20 (with REAL historical market odds)

The Odds API was upgraded to a paid plan (20K credits). This unlocked **backtestable market
odds for the 2022 World Cup** (their history starts June 2020, so 2018 is NOT available — 2022
is the only backtestable fold). Pulled pre-kickoff (~90 min before) no-vig consensus for all 48
2022 group matches across 32–35 books: `scripts/fetch_historical_odds_2022.py` →
`data/processed/market_features_2022.csv` (2,430 of 20,000 credits used).

## Finding 1 — the market is the benchmark; we do not beat it
`scripts/backtest_2022_market.py`, 2022 group stage, composite (lower better):

| model | RPS | LogLoss | composite |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B6 market consensus** | **0.224** | **1.037** | 0.3935 |
| Elo + market (50/50) | 0.231 | 1.061 | 0.3931 |
| B0 prior | 0.241 | 1.069 | 0.4038 |
| B1 Elo | 0.244 | 1.122 | 0.4157 |
| V8 candidate (pre-cycle-3, 0.5 blend) | 0.248 | 1.137 | 0.4194 |

- The no-vig market crushes every model on RPS/log-loss. Blends only "beat" it by 0.0004 on
  composite (pure draw-calibration noise on 48 matches). **No model beats the market.**
- **The 0.5-blend candidate was the WORST skill model** — worse than plain Elo and even the
  prior. This matched the 2026 prequential signal: the candidate was over-softened (the 50% Elo
  blend + logit pulled probabilities toward the middle, hurting sharpness).

## Finding 2 — fix: lean the candidate harder on Elo (cycle-3 accepted change)
Blend-weight sweep on the FIXED selection folds (2018 + 2022), governance-clean:

| ELO_BLEND_WEIGHT | dev+gate | 2018 | 2022 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.50 (old) | 0.3892 | 0.3591 | 0.4194 |
| 0.70 | 0.3857 | 0.3558 | 0.4157 |
| **0.85 (accepted)** | **0.3852** | **0.3541** | 0.4162 |
| 1.00 (pure Elo) | 0.3860 | 0.3562 | 0.4157 |

**Accepted: ELO_BLEND_WEIGHT 0.50 → 0.85.** Improves BOTH selection folds with no regression,
and edges pure Elo (a 15% logit touch helps draw calibration). After the change, the candidate's
2022 composite improves 0.4194 → 0.4162 (now equal to plain Elo) and is no longer the worst
model. Official 3-fold summary composite ≈ 0.398; 45 tests pass.
