2018); 2022 is the gate, 2026-MD1 is locked — **neither used for selection**. Objective

## Hypotheses tested (20 experiments, 2 branches)

C ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 1.0} × blend ∈ {0.80, 0.85, 0.90} × features ∈ {full, lean}.
- **Best dev J = 0.3539** (blend 0.90, C∈{0.5,1.0}) — only **0.0002** better than baseline, far below
  the 0.002 threshold → **REJECT** (noise; baking it in risks overfitting the sweep).
- **full ≡ lean** at every setting → the market + research-proxy feature columns are **inert (zero)
  in historical folds**; dropping them changes nothing. Documented, kept for forward compatibility.
- Blend 0.90 nudges the mean via the 2014 fold only; 0.85 remains the honest choice.

### Branch B — train-only draw recalibration (2 variants): `scripts/research_exp_draw.py`
Research priority #5; objective weights draws 35%.
- **E_draw_platt** (logistic recal of draw prob on train): dev J = **0.3579 (WORSE)**, dECE 0.0581→
  0.0676 → overfits train draw structure. **REJECT.**
- **E_draw_temp** (shrink draw toward train base rate): dev J = 0.3540 (−0.0001, noise) AND
  **regresses 2 of 3 dev folds** (2014, 2018). Fails no-regression criterion. **REJECT.**
